Getting Started with Statistics in Physical Therapy

A first step toward using statistics with confidence in PT practice and research.

What this course is about

Statistics is not only a collection of calculations. It is a language for learning from observations, expressing uncertainty, and deciding which scientific claims the available evidence can support.

The eight lessons in stats4PT follow that movement from observations toward claims. They connect statistical inference with causal reasoning, Bayesian updating, mechanisms, and the critical evaluation of research in physical therapy.

Before you begin

  • No advanced mathematics is required.
  • Read the lessons in order for the clearest progression.
  • Focus on the assumptions behind a method, not only its output.
  • Keep a clinical or research question in mind as you work.

The learning path

The course begins by asking how observations become statistical claims. It then distinguishes association from causation, introduces Bayesian reasoning and applications, examines when population evidence applies to an individual, makes causal assumptions visible with diagrams, and concludes with common fallacies and biases.
