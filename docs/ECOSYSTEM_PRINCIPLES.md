# Clinical Inquiry Ecosystem Principles

## Purpose

The projects in this ecosystem are related, but they are not interchangeable
stages of one educational product. Each has a distinct scholarly purpose and
owns a different part of the clinical inquiry process.

The boundaries below should guide site copy, navigation, lesson links,
integrations, diagrams, and future software connections.

## Conceptual architecture

```text
Observations and structured studies
                |
                v
     stats4PT: discovery scholarship
 language and methods of scientific inquiry
                |
                v
 evidence, estimates, uncertainty, and causal claims ----+
                                                        |
Physiology and other generative-mechanism knowledge     |
                |                                       |
                v                                       v
             Physiolog --------------------------> Models4PT
                                             integrative scholarship
                                    comprehensive population causal models
                                                        |
                                                        v
                                      Clinical Inference Engine
                                           practice scholarship
                                    patient-specific practice reasoning
```

This is not a simple linear curriculum. Stats4PT and Physiolog make different
contributions to model building. Models4PT performs the distinct integrative
work of combining admissible knowledge into comprehensive causal models. The
Clinical Inference Engine uses population knowledge with individual information
to support inspectable reasoning in practice.

## Project boundaries

### stats4PT: discovery scholarship

Using Boyer's model of scholarship, stats4PT is primarily concerned with
discovery. It teaches the language and methods of scientific inquiry used to
move from observations toward warranted claims.

stats4PT addresses:

- how observations become data and evidence;
- how samples are used to reason about populations;
- statistical inference, uncertainty, and estimation;
- Bayesian, inductive, and causal reasoning in scientific inquiry;
- the assumptions, biases, and fallacies that affect scientific claims; and
- how inquiry contributes evidence and candidate causal knowledge for models.

stats4PT may teach learners to represent or critique causal assumptions, but it
does not own the integrative process of building and curating comprehensive
population causal models. That responsibility belongs to Models4PT. It also
does not perform patient-specific practice reasoning, which belongs to the
Clinical Inference Engine.

### Physiolog: generative-mechanism knowledge

Physiolog develops and teaches physiology as a mechanistic foundation for
clinical reasoning. Its contribution to Models4PT is knowledge about
generative causal mechanisms and structures, helping models extend beyond
intervention-outcome associations.

Physiolog addresses:

- physiological systems and their relationships to movement and muscle
  function;
- mechanistic explanations of how and why effects may occur;
- interactive exploration of physiological behavior; and
- knowledge needed to represent causal depth in comprehensive models.

### Models4PT: integrative scholarship

Using Boyer's model, Models4PT is concerned with integrative scholarship. It
combines empirical evidence, statistical and causal claims, physiological
mechanisms, contextual structures, provenance, uncertainty, and disagreement
into curated population-level causal knowledge.

Model building is therefore a distinct integrative process. It is not merely
the final lesson or output of stats4PT.

Models4PT asks: **What do we collectively know?**

### Clinical Inference Engine: practice scholarship

Using Boyer's model, the Clinical Inference Engine is concerned with practice,
specifically practice reasoning. It combines population-derived causal
knowledge with information about an individual situation to support iterative,
inspectable belief revision and patient-specific reasoning.

The Clinical Inference Engine asks: **What follows here, for this person, in
this context?**

It should consume curated population knowledge without collapsing population
estimates into automatic diagnoses, prognoses, or treatment recommendations.
Clinical instantiation—the recursive construction of a provisional
patient-specific explanatory model from generic causal knowledge and evidence
about a particular patient—therefore belongs here. A future educational app or
game for constructing and revising instantiated models should be built from the
Clinical Inference Engine rather than embedded in stats4PT.

## Integration rules

1. Preserve project boundaries in every cross-link and diagram.
2. Describe stats4PT as contributing evidence and causal claims to model
   building, not as the educational path connecting all projects.
3. Describe Physiolog as contributing knowledge of generative mechanisms and
   structures to comprehensive models.
4. Describe Models4PT as the integrative model-building and knowledge-curation
   project.
5. Describe the Clinical Inference Engine as the patient-specific practice
   reasoning project.
6. Label whether an integration is educational content, a conceptual link, a
   shared knowledge representation, or a software/data interface.
7. Do not imply that a statistical association alone establishes a causal
   model or that a population model alone determines care for an individual.
8. Keep provenance, uncertainty, assumptions, and human judgment visible as
   knowledge moves between projects.

## Implications for the stats4PT site

- The homepage should position stats4PT as the language and methods of discovery
  within the broader ecosystem.
- The site should show two distinct contributions to Models4PT: scientific
  claims developed through inquiry and generative-mechanism knowledge developed
  through physiology.
- Links to Models4PT should be framed as a handoff to integrative scholarship,
  not as the next stats4PT lesson.
- Links to the Clinical Inference Engine should distinguish learning about
  Bayesian reasoning from performing patient-specific practice reasoning.
- Physiolog simulations may support stats4PT learning while remaining owned and
  maintained by Physiolog.
- Lesson integrations should state what is crossing the boundary: evidence,
  an assumption, a mechanism, a model, or a practice-reasoning example.
